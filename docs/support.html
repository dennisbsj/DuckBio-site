FAQ

What inspired the DuckBio apps?

The DuckBio apps are developed based on hands-on experience with scientific software used by large companies in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries. As these systems can be complex and unintuitive, DuckBio focuses on lightweight, practical apps that are easy for anyone to learn and use, regardless of prior experience.

Are the DuckBio apps validated for GMP use?

No. The apps are GMP-compatible, as they are designed to support GMP-regulated workflows. They can still easily be used in any settings. Organisations using DuckBio apps in a GMP setting are responsible for performing any required validation or qualification.

Is there a binding period?

No. Subscriptions are billed monthly, with no minimum commitment. You can cancel at any time, and your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.

What if my team has 20 or more users?

Contact us for a custom quote.

How do the free trials work?

Starting a trial in any DuckBio app will activate a single 14-day trial period for all seven apps. Access to all apps will be terminated after the 14 days.
