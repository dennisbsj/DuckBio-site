Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

Where can I see the six apps?

Visit the applications overview and open any product page.

How do I register for a trial?

Registration is not active yet. The planned launch offers one seven-day trial across all six apps.

How do I manage my subscription?

A secure, self-service Stripe Customer Portal is planned; no subscription portal is active.

Does DuckBio offer meetings or consulting?

No. DuckBio focuses on software products rather than consulting or sales meetings.

Is DuckBio certified for regulated use?

No blanket certification or compliance claim is made. Each application and its intended use must be assessed against the relevant requirements.

Need help?

Contact DuckBio.

Use the contact form on the homepage to write your enquiry. Message delivery will be activated when a verified DuckBio contact service is configured. The form will not claim a message has been sent before the service confirms receipt.
