FAQ

Where can I see the six apps?

Visit the apps overview and open any product page.

How do I register for a trial?

Registration is not active yet. The planned launch offers one seven-day trial across all six apps.

How do I manage my subscription?

A secure, self-service Stripe Customer Portal is planned; no subscription portal is active.

Does DuckBio offer meetings or consulting?

No. DuckBio focuses on software products rather than consulting or sales meetings.

Is DuckBio certified for regulated use?

No blanket certification or compliance claim is made. Each app and its intended use must be assessed against the relevant requirements.

Do all six apps cost the same?

Yes. Every individual app costs €35 per user per month. Mixed (2–5 apps) saves 20%, and all six apps cost €157.50 per user per month with the 25% Bundle discount, before any organisation discount. See the pricing page for other currencies.

Can users have different combinations of apps?

Yes. Different users can have different sets of apps. The Mixed plan offers a 20% bundle saving when a user selects 2–5 apps. The organisation discount is based on the total number of licensed users.

Do organisation discounts stack with app bundle savings?

Yes. The 20% Mixed or 25% Bundle saving is applied first. Eligible organisations then receive another 10% for 5–19 users or 20% for 20–49 users. Organisations with 50+ users receive a custom quotation.

Are the organisation discounts applied automatically?

Yes. Teams with 1–4 users pay the standard subscription rate; 5–19 users receive an extra 10%, and 20–49 users receive an extra 20% after bundle savings. Prices exclude VAT.

Are subscriptions and checkout live?

No. Checkout and payments are not yet active. You can select apps and review an indicative monthly price on the pricing page, but this does not place an order or start a subscription.

Can I try the software first?

A single seven-day trial across all six apps without payment details is planned. Registration and activation are not yet available.

Will I have to sign a long contract?

The proposed model uses monthly subscriptions. The final renewal and cancellation terms will be confirmed before checkout becomes available.

What if my team has 50 or more users?

Organisations with 50 or more licensed users will receive an individual quotation. Any calculator values for such organisations are indicative only.
