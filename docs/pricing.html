Billing questions

Know what to expect.

Are subscriptions active already?

No. DuckBio subscription checkout has not been connected to a secure backend.

Will my trial automatically become paid?

No. The proposed trial will end without charging anyone. A subscription would require an explicit purchase.

Are the Early Adopter prices lifetime discounts?

No. Under the proposed policy, introductory subscribers retain their lower price for a further 12 months after an announced regular price increase, subject to agreed terms and notice.
