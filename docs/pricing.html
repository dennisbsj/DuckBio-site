Frequently asked questions

Pricing and subscriptions.

Do all six apps cost the same?

Yes. The proposed standard price is kr 200 per user per app per month before discounts. The six apps are LabDuck, DuckNote, DuckTrain, MethDuck, CytoDuck and DuckMan.

Can users have different combinations of apps?

Yes. Each user can have their own set of applications. The multi-app discount is calculated per user, while the organisation discount is based on the total number of licensed users.

Can the early adopter and organisation discounts be combined?

No. Bundle discounts apply first. Afterward, the higher of the two eligible discounts applies. The early adopter promotion is 20% off for the first 12 months for subscriptions beginning 1 January through 31 March 2027.

Are subscriptions and checkout live?

No. This page describes planned 2027 pricing. Payment, account provisioning and automatic subscriptions are not available yet.

Can I try the software first?

A seven-day trial covering all six apps without payment details is planned. Trial activation is not available yet.

Will I have to sign a long contract?

The proposed model uses monthly subscriptions. Final renewal and cancellation terms will be confirmed before purchasing becomes available.

What if my team has 50 or more users?

Organisations with 50 or more licensed users receive an individual quote. The calculator shows only the bundle-adjusted baseline in that case.
