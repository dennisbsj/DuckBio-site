A few useful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Do all DuckBio apps cost the same?

Yes. The standard list price is kr 200 per user per app per month before any eligible discount. You can combine apps to unlock better multi-app pricing.

Can different people use different apps?

Yes. Each user may have their own combination of licensed apps. The suite discount is based on the number of apps assigned to that particular user; organisation discounts depend on total licensed users.

Can I combine discounts?

Multi-app discounts apply first. The higher of an eligible organisation discount or the time-limited 20% early adopter discount applies next. The latter two discounts never stack.

What happens after the early adopter period?

New subscriptions beginning after 31 March 2027 use standard pricing and any applicable organisation discount. Eligible early adopters retain their 20% promotional discount for the first 12 months of their subscription, after which standard applicable pricing applies.

Is there a long contract term?

We plan monthly subscriptions. Exact cancellation and subscription terms will be confirmed before checkout becomes available.

What if our organisation has 50 or more users?

Large organisations receive individual pricing. The calculator shows the baseline for comparison, but cannot determine your final negotiated rate.

Can I purchase an app here today?

Not yet. This is a preview of DuckBio's planned pricing structure. Checkout, account provisioning and payment integration will be added once tested and ready for launch.
